Iso-discomfort surfaces: NYC summer “feels like”, running pace

The Steadman AT formula (previous version) implicitly assumes something close to a resting body — it has no metabolic-rate input at all. A working muscle can put out 5–15× resting heat output, and in still air that extra heat has to leave almost entirely through sweat evaporation, which humidity throttles. Ambient wind stays fixed at 0 mph (worst case, no breeze); the slider runs on running pace for a 160 lb, 5′10″ man, 6:00–12:00 /mi, then jumps to one final stop: a 20:00 /mi gentle walk, treated here as ≈ no exertion (below). Pace → speed → VO2 → watts via the ACSM running equation (metabolic rate scales with body mass, not height), then feeds the same exertion-offset model as before — still a rule-of-thumb shift, not a solved heat-balance model (that would be WBGT / ISO 7933). A body moving through still air still feels wind — its own ground speed, right in its face — so a toggle below lets that self-generated airflow count even with zero ambient wind.

Base field: Steadman/Australian-BOM apparent temperature, AT = T + 0.33·e − 0.70·v − 4.0, e the vapor pressure from T and RH. v is ambient wind (0, fixed) plus — when the toggle is on — the runner's own ground speed, since moving through calm air creates the same relative airflow a breeze would. Pace→watts: speed = 1 mi / pace; VO2 (mL·kg⁻¹·min⁻¹) = 0.2·speed(m/min) + 3.5 (ACSM, level ground); METs = VO2/3.5; watts = METs × 3.5 × 72.6 kg ÷ 200 × 69.78 (72.6 kg = 160 lb; height doesn't enter — these equations scale with mass only). Exertion adds ΔAT = k(watts) × (0.35 + 0.65·RH/100) × 1/(1+0.06·vmph)°F, with k = max(0, (watts−100)/24): the humidity factor is evaporative cooling degrading as air nears saturation, and the last factor is relative airflow partly relieving that (diminishing returns, never all the way to zero — sweat still can't evaporate infinitely fast). This is one of several "feels like" formulas in use — the US NWS instead switches between a Heat Index (T + RH only) and Wind Chill (T + wind only), never a true 3D field. Box tailored to NYC conditions (50–100°F, 30–95% RH); the hot+humid corner (100°F at 95% RH) is rarer here than the box implies, since local dew points seldom exceed the upper 70s°F even at peak humidity. The final slider stop (20:00 /mi, a gentle walk, ≈3.3 MET / ≈290 W by the ACSM walking equation — roughly 3× resting, not literally zero) is deliberately pinned to zero exertion offset here: at that low an output, radiation and convection alone comfortably carry the extra heat even at 0 mph, so treating it as the "no exertion" baseline is a reasonable simplification for this tool, unlike real running paces where evaporation dominates and the shift is large. Treat all magnitudes as illustrative, not medical guidance. No external libraries — everything is computed and drawn locally.
